Click a column header to sort. EUR values are auto-scaled (k = thousand, m = million, bn = billion).
Source — Kosovo Banking Association: Monthly Reports on Financial Statements & Banking Statistics and Quarterly Banks' Financial Statements & Statistics reports. Figures are preliminary, IFRS-based and unaudited.
Method — Income and profit are cumulative year-to-date and reset each January, so they are not differenced against the prior period. ROA and ROE are as published by KBA (annualised on a six-period average denominator). Equity/assets is an unweighted leverage proxy, not a regulatory capital ratio. Bank figures are published rounded to EUR thousands, so component sums can differ from printed totals in the final digits.
Ranking — Ranks are derived from reported total assets. KBA's printed rank matches that ordering everywhere except 30/09/2024, where the published table kept IS Bank at 9 and PriBank at 11 after PriBank had already overtaken it; the printed value is retained in the data and shown on hover.
Comparability — The panel mixes quarterly releases (2024–2025) with monthly ones (2026), so consecutive periods are not equally spaced. Every step-based figure in the exported panel carries a step_months column; year-on-year figures are always month-matched and unaffected.
